Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Doppler is a prediction game you play with your friends using free points called Hz. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else handles it, and how to delete it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address and name that service shares with us (with Apple you can hide your email). Guests sign in without either; we only store a random account ID.
  • Your profile. The username and display name you choose.
  • What you do in the game. Groups you join, markets you pitch, bets, votes, disputes, evidence and notes you post, Hz balance and history, daily check-ins, and reports or blocks you make.
  • Notification details. If you allow notifications, a push token for your device and your notification settings (such as muted groups and the streak reminder).

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (unless you choose to attach one as evidence), payment details, or advertising identifiers. There are no ads and no third-party analytics in the app.

Why we use it

  • To run the game: show your groups, settle markets, pay out Hz and keep the leaderboard.
  • To send the notifications you allow.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: prevent abuse, review reports and enforce the community rules.

Who can see it

  • Your groups. Members of a group see your username, display name, your bets and positions in that group's markets, what you pitch and post there, and your place on its leaderboard. Jury votes stay hidden until the vote is counted.
  • Nobody else in the app. There are no public profiles.
  • Reports go to the Doppler team only. The person you report is not told who reported them.

Services that handle data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in, in Singapore (AWS ap-southeast-1).
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them.
  • Expo, Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging deliver notifications. They receive your push token and the notification text.

We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app: You → Settings → Delete account. Deleting removes your sign-in, email, profile details, push tokens and group memberships straight away. Bets and results you were part of stay in your friends' markets so their payouts still add up, but they show as "Deleted player" and can no longer be linked to you.

Guests can delete their account the same way. If you uninstall the app as a guest without deleting, the account becomes unreachable; write to us and we will remove it.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by writing to mauviclim+doppler@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. If you are in Singapore, the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 applies; elsewhere you may have similar rights under your local law.

Age

Doppler is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly allow anyone younger to use it, and we delete accounts we learn belong to someone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
