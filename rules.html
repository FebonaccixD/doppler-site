Community rules

Last updated 9 November 2026

Doppler is for friendly calls about your friends and your lives. Keep it the kind of thing the person it's about would laugh at too.

Don't pitch markets about

  • Anyone's death, injury, illness or self-harm.
  • Anyone under 18.
  • People who aren't in the group and haven't agreed to be talked about, such as a friend's ex, a coworker or a stranger.
  • Someone's sex life, health, religion, sexuality or other private matters.
  • Crimes, or anything that would encourage someone to do something dangerous or illegal to win.

Don't post

  • Harassment, bullying, threats or hateful content.
  • Sexual content.
  • Other people's private information, such as addresses, phone numbers or private photos.
  • Spam or scams.

Play fair

  • One account per person.
  • Don't fix the outcome of a market you bet on, and don't team up to throw votes.
  • Be honest when you propose a result or vote on a jury.

If something's wrong

Tap ··· on a market or tap a player's name, then Report. Reports come to us, not to the group, and we look at each one as soon as we can. You can also Block someone: you stop seeing their markets, and they can't bet on yours or on markets about you.

Group admins can take down markets and remove members. We can remove content and close accounts that break these rules.
